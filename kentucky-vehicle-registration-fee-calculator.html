How much will my tag and registration cost in Kentucky?

For most Kentucky drivers, the flat state registration fee for a passenger car is just $21.00 per year—making it one of the lowest base rates in the nation. However, Kentucky assesses an annual motor vehicle property tax at the exact same time you renew your plates. This tax is based on your vehicle's NADA trade-in value, so a newer vehicle will cost significantly more to renew than an older one. Additionally, if you drive a fully electric vehicle or plug-in hybrid, expect an annual surcharge of $126.

How are fees calculated in Kentucky

Your total bill at the county clerk’s office is determined by combining the flat state administration fees with value-based local and state taxes:

  • Base Renewal Fee: A flat $21.00 for passenger vehicles and light trucks, and $18.50 for motorcycles.
  • Ad Valorem Property Tax: An annual tax collected by the clerk on behalf of your local taxing districts (schools, city, county). It averages about 40 to 60 cents per $100 of the vehicle's value.
  • Motor Vehicle Usage Tax (MVUT): A one-time 6% tax collected when you purchase and title a vehicle in Kentucky (similar to a sales tax).
  • EV & PHEV Surcharge: To offset lost gas tax revenue, Kentucky levies an annual road-use fee of $126 for battery-electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles (or $63 for electric motorcycles).

What affects your registration in Kentucky?

  • Vehicle Value: Because of the property tax, luxury cars and brand-new trucks will carry much higher renewal costs than older, depreciated sedans.
  • Transaction Needs: Financing a new car adds a $22.00 Lien Filing Fee. Needing a speed title adds $25.00. Replacing lost decals adds $6.00.
  • Powertrain: Buying a plug-in hybrid or electric car immediately adds $126 to your baseline renewal cost every year.
  • Notarizations: Kentucky requires many transfer forms to be notarized. The clerk charges $2.00 per stamp if they do it for you at the counter.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Kentucky?

The standard annual base registration fee for a passenger car is $21.00. However, the total amount due at renewal will also include your annual motor vehicle property tax, which is calculated based on the vehicle's trade-in value.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Kentucky?

This tool is highly accurate for calculating checkout totals if you manually input the correct Usage Tax (6% of retail) and Property Tax figures from your official state notices. It natively supports the exact fee structures for standard passenger cars, motorcycles, and the 2026 electric vehicle surcharges.

Can registration fees in Kentucky change every year?

Yes. While the $21 flat fee rarely changes, the property tax portion of your bill decreases every year as your vehicle depreciates in value.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Kentucky?

Yes. If you are registering a newly purchased car (new or used), simply enter 6% of the purchase price into the "Motor Vehicle Usage Tax" field and select "Yes" under the "Title Application" field to get a complete estimate of your first-time registration cost.
