How much are vehicle registration fees in maryland?

For most passenger cars, Maryland charges a biennial (2-year) registration fee based on the vehicle's shipping weight. Vehicles 3,500 lbs or less pay $241.00 for a two-year plate, while vehicles between 3,501 and 3,700 lbs pay $251.00. Heavier passenger vehicles (like SUVs and trucks) over 3,700 lbs jump to $383.00 for two years. These rates went into effect during recent legislative changes and include the mandatory Emergency Medical Services (EMS) surcharge. If you drive an electric vehicle, you'll also pay an annual surcharge of $125 ($250 upfront for a 2-year tag).

What affects your maryland registration fee?

  • Vehicle Weight: Maryland tiers its passenger vehicle fees strictly by shipping weight. Heavy SUVs cost significantly more to renew than compact sedans.
  • Powertrain: To offset lost gas tax revenue, Maryland imposes a $125 annual surcharge on Zero Emission Vehicles (ZEVs) and a $100 annual surcharge on Plug-in Hybrids (PHEVs).
  • Transaction Type: A simple renewal just incurs the plate fee. First-time registrations will incur a $200 certificate of title fee.
  • Excise Tax: If purchasing a vehicle, you owe a one-time 6.5% excise tax on the purchase price. New residents transferring a vehicle pay either the difference between their previous state's tax rate and 6.5%, or a minimum $100 flat fee.

How are registration fees calculated in maryland?

The formula is mostly flat-fee based depending on your vehicle's class and weight, combined with the one-time 6.5% vehicle excise tax for new purchases.

  • Formula: Base Plate Fee (by weight) + EMS Surcharge + EV/PHEV Surcharge (if applicable) = Total Registration Fee.

For a new car purchase, the MVA calculates the 6.5% excise tax using the total purchase price (less any trade-in allowance), with a minimum taxable floor of $640.

What this calculator may not include

  • The $30 VEIP (Vehicle Emissions Inspection Program) test fee, which applies every two years for residents in the 14 participating counties.
  • Temporary tag fees, dealer processing charges (which are capped at $800 in Maryland), or specialty plate manufacturing costs.
  • Safety inspection fees, which are paid directly to private authorized inspection stations (usually $100–$200), not the MVA.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in maryland?

For a standard two-year registration, it costs $241.00 for vehicles 3,500 lbs and under, $251.00 for vehicles 3,501 to 3,700 lbs, and $383.00 for vehicles over 3,700 lbs. Electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles owe an additional $250 or $200 two-year surcharge, respectively.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in maryland?

This tool is highly accurate for passenger cars, light trucks, motorcycles, and standard trailers under 20,000 lbs. It incorporates the heavily updated 2026 MVA fee schedules. It does not cover heavy commercial fleet vehicles, historical plates, or farm equipment.

Can registration fees in maryland change every year?

Maryland's base registration fees do not depreciate as your vehicle ages. The plate renewal cost you pay in year two will be the same cost you pay in year ten. However, the state legislature does occasionally adjust the base fee brackets and EMS surcharges, as they did heavily for 2025/2026.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in maryland?

Yes. For both new and used vehicles purchased in-state, select "Title + Registration" and input the purchase price to calculate your 6.5% excise tax. If you are a new resident moving to Maryland, select the "New Maryland Resident" option to calculate your prorated excise tax credit.
