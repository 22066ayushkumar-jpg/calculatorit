How much are vehicle registration fees in Missouri?

In Missouri, the base registration fee for a modern passenger car (72 taxable horsepower or more) is $51.25 per year, plus a standard $9.00 processing fee. The state allows drivers to choose between a 1-year or 2-year registration term (which simply doubles the base fee and processing fee). If you are titling a newly purchased vehicle, you will also owe an $8.50 title fee, a $9.00 title processing fee, a $2.00 electronic transmission fee, and state and local sales taxes on the net purchase price of the vehicle.

What affects your Missouri registration fee?

  • Taxable Horsepower: Missouri is unique in that passenger car registration is tiered by engine horsepower. While very small or older vehicles might fall into the $24.25 or $33.25 brackets, almost all modern cars, trucks, and SUVs fall into the top bracket (72+ HP) and cost $51.25 annually.
  • Powertrain / EV Decals: Missouri does not charge a gas tax on electricity. To make up the difference, Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) must purchase a Special Fuel Decal annually. By 2026, the passenger BEV decal reached its final phased-in cost of $150.00, while Plug-in Hybrids (PHEVs) pay $75.00.
  • Sales Tax and Trade-ins: When titling a new car, you must pay Missouri's 4.225% state sales tax plus any local county/city taxes. Missouri calculates this on the net purchase price, meaning the value of any trade-in vehicle is strictly deducted from your taxable total, saving you hundreds of dollars.

How are registration fees calculated in Missouri?

The formula the DOR uses combines your horsepower-based state fee with administrative processing fees for each individual line item.

  • Formula (Standard Renewal): (HP Base Rate × Term) + ($9 Processing × Term) + EV Decal Fees
  • Formula (New Title): (HP Base Rate × Term) + ($9 Reg Processing × Term) + $8.50 Title Fee + $9 Title Processing + $2 Electronic Fee + Sales Tax

What this calculator may not include

  • County Personal Property Tax: Missouri assesses a significant annual personal property tax on vehicles based on their market value. You MUST pay this bill to your county collector and obtain a tax receipt before the DOR will let you renew your registration.
  • Safety and Emissions Inspections: Vehicles over 10 model years old or with more than 150,000 miles require a biennial safety inspection ($12 statutory limit). Residents of the St. Louis area (St. Louis City, St. Louis County, St. Charles, Franklin, and Jefferson counties) also require an emissions test ($24).

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Missouri?

The state base registration fee for most modern cars (72 HP or more) is $51.25 for a one-year term or $102.50 for a two-year term, plus a required $9.00 or $18.00 processing fee, respectively.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Missouri?

This calculator is highly accurate for passenger cars, motorcycles, and standard light trucks up to 12,000 lbs using the 2026 DOR fee schedule. It does not calculate heavy commercial fleet registrations, permanent trailer plates, or boat/vessel taxes.

Can registration fees in Missouri change every year?

The base registration fees rarely change. However, if you drive an electric vehicle or plug-in hybrid, the state has been increasing the Special Fuel Decal fee by 20% each year until it reached $150 (for BEVs) in 2026. Your separate county personal property tax will also decrease annually as the vehicle depreciates.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Missouri?

Yes. Set the transaction type to "New Purchase (Initial Title & Registration)" for any newly acquired vehicle to calculate the sales tax and titling fees. If you are just renewing your plates on a car you already own, select "Annual Tag Renewal."
