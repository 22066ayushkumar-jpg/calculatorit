How much will my tag and registration cost in Indiana?

For a standard passenger vehicle, the fixed BMV base registration fee in Indiana is extremely low at just $21.35, plus a mandatory $15.00 Transportation Infrastructure Improvement Fee (TIIF). However, the real cost of registering your vehicle is driven by the annual vehicle excise tax. This tax is based on your vehicle's original retail value (MSRP) and its age, meaning a brand-new $35,000 SUV could cost over $400 to register, while an eight-year-old compact car might cost less than $70 total.

How are fees calculated in Indiana

The Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV) calculates your annual registration by stacking state flat fees, local flat fees, and a value-based tax schedule:

  • Base Registration & TIIF: A fixed $21.35 standard passenger fee plus a fixed $15.00 TIIF fee applies to all vehicles.
  • Vehicle Excise Tax: Indiana groups vehicles into 17 value "classes" based on original MSRP. The tax begins high in Year 1 and declines every year until the vehicle is 10 years old, at which point it bottoms out (between $12 and $50).
  • County / Municipal Taxes: Also known as a Wheel Tax or Surtax, these are enacted at the local level. Some counties charge a flat $25, while others charge a percentage of the excise tax.
  • Supplemental Fuel Fees: To make up for lost gas tax revenue, fully electric vehicles (BEVs) are assessed an annual $242.00 surcharge, while hybrids and plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) are assessed an $81.00 surcharge.

What affects your registration in Indiana?

  • Vehicle Age & Original Value: This dictates your excise tax. A luxury car will stay in a higher excise tax bracket for far longer than an economy car.
  • Your Home County: Because local municipalities can enact wheel and surtaxes, registering the exact same car in Marion County might cost more than registering it in a neighboring rural county.
  • Powertrain: EV and Hybrid surcharges add significant fixed costs to your yearly renewal.
  • Timeliness: Indiana imposes steep administrative penalties if you are late. A late registration adds $15, and a late title application adds $30.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Indiana?

The flat registration fee is $21.35 plus a $15.00 infrastructure fee, totaling $36.35. However, nearly all owners also must pay an annual excise tax that ranges from $12 to $532 depending on the vehicle's age and original price.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Indiana?

This calculator is accurate for calculating your total checkout price, provided you have your specific excise and local tax numbers from your BMV renewal notice. It supports standard passenger cars, motorcycles, light trucks, trailers, and RVs.

Can registration fees in Indiana change every year?

Yes, your total cost will change every year. Because Indiana's excise tax is based on a declining age scale, your registration renewal should get slightly cheaper every year until the vehicle is 10 years old.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Indiana?

Yes. If you are buying a car, just change the "Need a New Title?" dropdown to "Yes". Be aware that if you are buying a used car, the excise tax will be significantly lower than it is for a brand-new car, so adjust the Excise Tax input field accordingly.
