How much are vehicle registration fees in Maine?

The baseline state registration fee for a passenger vehicle in Maine is a flat $35.00 per year. However, this is only a fraction of what you will actually pay. Before you can register your vehicle with the state, Maine law requires you to pay an annual motor vehicle excise tax to your local municipality. For a brand new $30,000 car, this local tax adds $720 to your bill in the first year. First-time registrations will also include a $33.00 title fee and a $6.00 municipal agent processing fee.

How are registration fees calculated in Maine?

The formula for Maine vehicle registration relies on combining the flat state Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV) fee with your local municipal excise tax. The excise tax is strictly based on the vehicle's original Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP)—not your purchase price—and depreciates over six years.

The formula the calculator uses for excise tax is: MSRP × Mil Rate.

The municipal mil rates per $1,000 of MSRP are:

  • Year 1: 24.00 mils (2.40%)
  • Year 2: 17.50 mils (1.75%)
  • Year 3: 13.50 mils (1.35%)
  • Year 4: 10.00 mils (1.00%)
  • Year 5: 6.50 mils (0.65%)
  • Year 6 and older: 4.00 mils (0.40%)

What affects your Maine registration fee?

  • Original MSRP: The higher the original window sticker price of the vehicle, the higher your excise tax will be. A $60,000 truck will cost twice as much to register as a $30,000 sedan.
  • Vehicle Age: The excise tax rate drops significantly each year for the first six years of the vehicle's life. By year six, the rate drops to just $4.00 per $1,000 of value, effectively bottoming out.
  • Vehicle Type: Motorcycles, mopeds, and trailers have lower base state registration fees than passenger cars. Commercial trucks have weight-based fees not covered by this standard calculator.

What this calculator may not include

  • Maine state sales tax (5.5%), which is collected at the time of first registration if you purchased the vehicle from a private party or an out-of-state dealer.
  • Specialty license plate fees (Agriculture, Conservation, University plates, etc.) which usually add an initial $20 fee and a $15 annual renewal fee.
  • Additional excise taxes for commercial vehicles over 26,000 lbs.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Maine?

The state charges a flat $35 for passenger car registration. However, you must also pay a municipal excise tax based on the vehicle's MSRP. For newer vehicles, this local tax can easily exceed $500 to $1,000.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Maine?

This calculator is highly accurate for standard passenger vehicles, motorcycles, and light trailers, assuming you input the exact original MSRP. It does not calculate weight-based commercial truck fees or apportioned registrations.

Can registration fees in Maine change every year?

Yes. Your total renewal cost will decrease every year for the first six years of the vehicle's life as the excise mil rate drops. After the 6th year, the excise tax reaches its permanent floor of 4 mils.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Maine?

Yes. For a new car, set the vehicle age to "Year 1" and ensure "Title Transaction" is set to "Certificate of Title". For a used car, select the appropriate age bracket to calculate the depreciated excise tax.
