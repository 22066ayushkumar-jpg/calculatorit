How much are vehicle registration fees in Mississippi?

In Mississippi, the flat state registration fee (which consists of the base tag fee plus the privilege license tax) is extremely low—typically just $29.00 for a new passenger car or $27.75 for a renewal. However, Mississippi drivers are also required to pay an annual ad valorem tax based on their county's millage rate and the vehicle's depreciated assessed value. For a newer vehicle, this tax is by far the largest portion of your tag renewal bill, frequently ranging from $400 to $900.

What affects your Mississippi registration fee?

  • Original MSRP: The state bases its 30% assessed valuation strictly on the manufacturer's suggested retail price, not the price you paid for it.
  • Vehicle Age: The ad valorem tax drops each year according to a 10-year depreciation schedule. A 10-year-old vehicle will be assessed at just 10% of its original value.
  • County Millage Rate: Because ad valorem taxes fund local schools and services, where you live drastically changes your bill. High-millage areas like Hinds County will cost significantly more than lower-millage rural counties.
  • Electric Vehicles: EVs and Plug-in Hybrids (PHEVs) must pay a special $150 or $75 annual road-use surcharge to offset gas tax losses.
  • Sales Tax: If you are registering a newly purchased vehicle from a private party or an out-of-state dealer, you will pay a 5% state sales or use tax on the net purchase price directly to the county collector.

How are registration fees calculated in Mississippi?

The formula Mississippi county tax collectors use combines flat state fees and local value-based taxes. The base fee is simply the state registration rate ($14.00) plus the privilege tax ($15.00 for cars).

The Ad Valorem tax is calculated using this formula:

  • Assessed Value = (MSRP × 30%) × Depreciation Factor
  • Gross Tax = Assessed Value × (Mill Rate ÷ 1000)

Mississippi then applies a Legislative Tag Credit (typically 5% of the assessed value) to reduce your final tax bill, thanks to a fund maintained by the state's sales tax revenues.

What this calculator may not include

  • Local municipal/city taxes that apply in addition to the county millage rates if you live inside certain city limits.
  • Specialty license plate fees (vanity plates, collegiate plates, military tags) which carry additional initial and annual renewal fees.
  • Penalties for registering or renewing a tag after the deadline (which can reach up to 25% of the total tax depending on delinquency length).

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Mississippi?

The fixed state portion for a passenger car is just $12.75 for a renewal and $14.00 for an initial registration, plus a $15.00 privilege tax. However, the total cost you pay at the county tax office will be much higher due to the mandatory ad valorem property tax.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Mississippi?

This calculator is highly accurate for standard passenger cars, light trucks (under 10,000 lbs), and motorcycles using the state's 10-year depreciation schedule and 30% assessment rule. It does not calculate heavy commercial fleet registrations or multi-axle trailers.

Can registration fees in Mississippi change every year?

Yes. Your ad valorem tax will decrease every year for the first 10 years of your vehicle's life as the vehicle depreciates. Additionally, your county may adjust its millage rate from year to year to fund local budgets.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Mississippi?

Yes. For a new car, set the transaction type to "New Purchase" and the age to "1st Year". For a used car, simply choose the correct vehicle age bracket to calculate the properly depreciated ad valorem tax. If you bought it from a private seller, you can also use the sales tax field to estimate your 5% use tax.
