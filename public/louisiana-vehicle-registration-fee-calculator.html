How much are vehicle registration fees in Louisiana?

For most passenger cars and light trucks, Louisiana charges an annualized rate of 0.1% of the vehicle's original selling value. However, since passenger plates are renewed every two years, you actually pay 0.2% of the value at the time of registration. The state sets a minimum vehicle value of $10,000, meaning the absolute minimum you will pay for a two-year passenger plate is $20.00. First-time registrations will also include a $68.50 title fee and an $8.00 handling fee.

How are registration fees calculated in Louisiana?

The formula for passenger vehicles in Louisiana is straightforward. The Office of Motor Vehicles (OMV) takes the vehicle's assessed value (with a $10,000 minimum floor) and multiplies it by 0.1% per year.

  • Formula: (Vehicle Value × 0.001) × 2 Years

If you are registering a heavy truck, motorcycle, or trailer, the state abandons the value-based formula and charges a flat fee based on weight or vehicle type instead. For example, a heavy truck between 6,001 and 10,000 lbs pays a flat $112.00 for a 4-year plate.

What affects your Louisiana registration fee?

  • Vehicle Value: This is the primary driver for passenger cars. A $50,000 vehicle costs $100 to register for two years, while a $15,000 vehicle costs $30. Unlike some states, this is typically based on the original selling price and does not decrease as the vehicle ages.
  • Vehicle Type: Motorcycles, motorhomes, and trailers all have specific flat fees instead of value-based fees, and are often issued on 4-year cycles instead of 2-year cycles.
  • Powertrain / Fuel Type: Electric vehicles incur a $110 annual road usage fee, and hybrid vehicles incur a $60 fee, to offset the fact that they do not pay traditional fuel taxes at the pump. Note for 2026: The OMV assumes direct collection of these road usage fees at the time of registration, meaning you will pay multiple years of this surcharge upfront depending on your plate's lifespan.
  • Transaction Type: First-time registrations will include a $68.50 title fee and an $8.00 handling fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Louisiana?

The standard passenger registration fee is calculated at 0.1% of the vehicle's selling price per year, billed in two-year increments (effectively 0.2%). A $30,000 car would cost $60 to register for two years. A minimum floor applies, so no passenger car pays less than $20.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Louisiana?

This calculator covers all standard passenger vehicles, light and heavy trucks (up to 10,000 lbs), motorcycles, motorhomes, and various trailer classes using the official OMV fee schedule. It does not calculate heavy commercial fleet registrations.

Can registration fees in Louisiana change every year?

While the 0.1% base formula for passenger cars remains static, Louisiana recently added new recurring fees for EV and Hybrid vehicles ($110 and $60 annually) to account for road usage. Also note that state sales tax rates applied at the initial time of registration can fluctuate.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Louisiana?

Yes. Simply ensure you enter the correct vehicle value and select whether you need a new title transaction. However, be aware that you will also owe state and local sales tax on the purchase of the vehicle, which this tool does not calculate.
