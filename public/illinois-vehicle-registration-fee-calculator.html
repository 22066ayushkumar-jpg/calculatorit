How much will my tag and registration cost in Illinois?

For standard passenger vehicles and B-trucks (8,000 lbs or less), the annual license plate renewal fee in Illinois is a flat $151. If you drive a battery electric vehicle (BEV), an additional $100 EV surcharge brings your annual renewal to $251. If you are registering a newly acquired car, you must also factor in the $165 title fee, making the standard total $316 for new registrations.

How are fees calculated in Illinois

Illinois Secretary of State (ILSOS) determines vehicle registration fees primarily based on vehicle classification and gross weight rather than vehicle value or model year:

  • Standard Passenger Cars & B-Trucks: Flat $151 annual registration fee.
  • Motorcycles: Flat $41 annual registration fee.
  • Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV): Standard $151 base fee plus a mandatory $100 annual EV surcharge ($251 total).
  • Titling Fees: A one-time $165 fee for new or transferred titles; $50 for duplicate or corrected titles.
  • Plate Transfers: A $25 transfer fee when moving valid license plates to another vehicle.
  • Recreational Vehicles & Trailers: Tiered flat rates based on gross weight ratings (ranging from $18 for light trailers up to $102 for large motorhomes).

What affects your registration in Illinois?

  • Vehicle Classification: Commercial C-trucks ($218) and heavier motorhomes carry higher base rates than standard passenger cars ($151).
  • Fuel Type / Powertrain: Pure electric vehicles carry a $100 annual surcharge to support state road funds in lieu of gas taxes.
  • Transaction Type: Transferring existing valid plates ($25) is significantly cheaper upfront than issuing brand-new plates ($151).
  • Timeliness: Renewing a expired registration more than 30 days late triggers a $20 late penalty fee.

What this calculator may not include

  • Sales / Use Taxes: Dealership sales tax or Illinois Department of Revenue RUT-50 / RUT-25 private party transaction taxes.
  • Local City Stickers: Municipal wheel taxes, such as the Chicago City Vehicle Sticker ($100+ annually).
  • Specialty & Vanity License Plates: Personalized, military, or organizational specialty plate initialization and renewal fees.
  • Emissions Inspection Fees: While Illinois air testing is free for qualifying vehicles, repair costs needed to pass testing before renewal are separate.

FAQS

How much is the car registration fee in Illinois?

The standard annual registration renewal fee for a regular passenger car, SUV, or B-truck (up to 8,000 lbs) in Illinois is $151.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Illinois?

This calculator covers standard passenger vehicles, B/C trucks, motorcycles, recreational trailers, and RVs using official ILSOS fee rates. Commercial fleets and heavy trucks over 10,000 lbs operate under separate mileage or gross-weight schedules.

Can registration fees in Illinois change every year?

Registration fees remain set at fixed statutory rates unless altered by state legislation. Fee structures like the $151 base tag fee and $100 EV surcharge are established by Illinois state law and apply consistently year to year.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Illinois?

Yes. Illinois charges the same flat $151 registration fee regardless of whether a car is brand-new or 20 years old. If you bought a car (new or used), simply select "Yes" under Title / Transfer Needed to add the required $165 title fee.
