How much are vehicle registration fees in massachusetts?

The standard passenger vehicle registration fee in Massachusetts is a flat $60.00, which covers you for a two-year period. Motorcycles are cheaper at $20.00 for a one-year plate. If you are registering a newly purchased vehicle, you must also pay a $75.00 certificate of title fee, along with the state's 6.25% motor vehicle sales and use tax, directly to the RMV at the time of registration.

What affects your massachusetts registration fee?

  • Plate Type: A standard passenger plate is $60. Upgrading to a vanity plate increases the cost to $80, and a "reserved" low-number plate costs $100.
  • Sales Tax Valuation: When buying a used car from a private party, the RMV compares your written purchase price to the vehicle's NADA "clean trade-in" value. You must pay 6.25% on whichever number is higher.
  • Transaction Needs: If you are transferring plates from an old car to a new one, there is a $25 transfer fee. A duplicate registration certificate costs $25.
  • Gifts: If you receive a vehicle as a gift from an immediate family member, you are exempt from the 6.25% sales tax, but you must pay a $25 gift transfer fee instead.

How are registration fees calculated in massachusetts?

The RMV uses a straightforward flat-fee system for the registration and title itself. The formula gets complicated when calculating the mandatory sales tax during a new title transfer.

  • Formula: Plate Fee + Title Fee ($75) + Sales Tax (6.25% of Max[Purchase Price, NADA Value]) + Admin Fees = Total RMV Cost.

Additionally, Massachusetts residents are subject to an annual motor vehicle excise tax billed by their local city or town, not the RMV. This is calculated at $25 per $1,000 of the vehicle's depreciated valuation.

What this calculator may not include

  • The mandatory Massachusetts vehicle safety and emissions inspection fee ($35.00), which you must pay at an authorized inspection station within 7 days of registering the car.
  • Your annual local municipal excise tax bill (unless you manually enter an estimate in the optional field).
  • Late fees or penalties for failing to register or title a vehicle within the RMV's required timeframe.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in massachusetts?

A standard passenger vehicle registration costs $60.00 and is valid for two years. If you are also titling the vehicle for the first time, add a $75.00 title fee, plus applicable 6.25% sales tax.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in massachusetts?

This calculator covers standard passenger cars, electric vehicles, motorcycles, and mopeds using the official May 2025 RMV fee schedule. It does not calculate weight-based commercial plates, apportioned registrations, or specific camper/trailer fees.

Can registration fees in massachusetts change every year?

The base registration fee ($60 for two years) does not change based on vehicle age or value. However, the separate annual motor vehicle excise tax (billed by your town) does depreciate and change each year.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in massachusetts?

Yes. Just select "New Purchase (Title + Registration)" and enter your purchase price. If it is a used car purchased from a private party, be sure to also enter the NADA trade-in value, as the RMV will tax you on whichever number is higher.
